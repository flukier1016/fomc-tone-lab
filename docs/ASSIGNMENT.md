# The assignment, line by line

IEOR 4723 final project, topic 4: **central bank communication index and duration positioning.** Build a hawkish-dovish index from Federal Reserve communication, score it with Claude against a defined rubric with evidence, compare it with simpler text baselines, test it against Treasury yields across the curve, and use it to tilt duration in a bond portfolio. The brief emphasizes rapid statement repricing and asks whether minutes, speeches or the change between consecutive statements offer information a later duration trade can use. Daily data can test the specified associations and trades, but cannot establish the exact speed of full pricing.

This page maps each requirement to what we built, where it lives, where to see it, and what came out. Research results are from the store and were frozen on 21 October 2026. `make run` preserves the frozen study while operational data can update and the forward test applies the fixed rules to new observations; the dashboard reads the same store. Slide references below point to the single integrated 46-slide deck, which includes both the introduction and technical methods.

## The hypotheses, as stated before the results

| | Hypothesis | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Claude labels tone closer to people than the baselines | Supported on the public expert labels (p = 0.004 against the closest baseline). The post-cutoff human test was not run. The public labels may be in the model's training data; masked and raw scores agree (r = 0.99), which partly addresses, but does not eliminate, this concern |
| H2 | Tone is associated with Treasury yield changes over the release window | Supported as association evidence: 26 of 198 tests survive false-discovery control; the daily design does not identify a causal communication shock |
| H3 | A pre-registered next-day duration tilt beats chance (pre-registered) | Not supported: Sharpe −0.18, placebo p = 0.28 (re-run once after a code review; see DESIGN.md) |
| H4 | Scores do not come from remembering the documents | Mixed: Claude recognizes the statements, but masked scores track raw ones (r = 0.99) |
